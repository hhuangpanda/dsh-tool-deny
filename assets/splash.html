dsh-tool-deny

Hide or refuse tools per agent preset in DeepSeek Harness.

# cordis.patch.yml
- insert:
    - id: tool-deny-root
      name: dsh-tool-deny
      config:
        hideByPreset:
          minimal:
            - image_search
            - translate
